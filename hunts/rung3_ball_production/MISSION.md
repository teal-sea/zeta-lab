# MISSION: `rung3_ball_production`, pricing the kernel check of the Davenport-Heilbronn zero

**Opened 2026-10-21. Hunt #145.** This is the `rung3-pilot` lane of the campaign the owner
directed on 2026-10-21 (route 3, "Rung 3: kernel-checked Davenport-Heilbronn zero"). It is
a ledger hunt: it pilots, measures and asks for a production window. It does not run
production. Nothing in this directory is a result until the case log in `hunts/README.md`
says how it ended.

The task text proposed number #138. The workflow that launched this lane reserved #138 to
#144 for landings it is renumbering, and assigned #145; on 2026-10-21 no ref under
`refs/heads` or `refs/remotes/origin` used #145 in its `hunts/README.md`.

Grades used here: *measured* (one route), *hardened* (independent routes agree, or ball
enclosures carry every step) and *kernel-checked* (Lean, zero `sorry`, only `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`). The reserved enclosure word belongs to
`zeta/rigor.py` and the Lean arm and appears nowhere in this directory.

## The question

> Does the 215-site ball certificate `lean/cert/rung3_plan2.json` compile at Lean
> `v4.35.0-rc2` on the swarm box, at what measured cost per prime-tower atom, per
> composite atom and per module, and does the kernel refuse a planted fault in an emitted
> radius and in `eps'`?

## Why it matters

`davenport_heilbronn_statement` (`lean/ZetaLean/DavenportHeilbronn.lean`, line 96) says
that some entire function with the Davenport-Heilbronn Dirichlet series and functional
equation has a zero off the critical line. Its analytic half (`dh_analytic_half`), the
tail bounds, the ball arithmetic and the square criterion (the theorem at line 185 of
`lean/ZetaLean/DHZeroCriterion.lean`, cited by line because its name carries the word this
directory may not use) are kernel-checked. What is missing is the numeric half: about half
a million literal obligations, one site theorem per point or box of plan v2, then one
assembly module. This hunt prices the first part and names the second.

## Starting point (as recorded on `main` at `fb647bfc7`; these are citations)

- **Plan v2** (`lean/cert/rung3_plan2.json`): 215 sites, 110 big boxes, 104 grid points
  and the centre. Small square `w = 3/64`, `eps' = 1/2000`, big square `w2 = 7/32`,
  `L = 16`, `M = 25499/10000`.
- **Mirror.** `scripts/65_rung3_full_validation.py` runs every site through the bit-exact
  Fraction ball mirror (`scripts/63_rung3_ball_mirror.py`); the record says all 215 pass at
  plan v2's geometry. `tests/test_rung3_mirror.py` and `tests/test_rung3_ball_assembly.py`
  check the mirror against mpmath and against the Hurwitz route.
- **Budget.** `scripts/67_rung3_ball_atom_budget.py` counts 549,149 atoms (468,240 tower
  atoms, 80,909 others) and prices them at 175.0 core-hours, atom-only and serial, from
  1.2504 CPU-s per tower atom and 0.55 CPU-s per composite atom. The tower figure was
  measured on 2026-09-17, when `lean/lean-toolchain` pinned `v4.33.0-rc2`, on a machine the
  record does not name. Both figures are pinned by `tests/test_rung3_ball_atom_budget.py`.
- **Driver.** `scripts/72_rung3_ball_compile.py` compiles a sharded site with
  `lake env lean --root`, checkpointing each module. The 2026-09-18 centre pilot stopped
  on an import that resolved beside the sources; commits `1e3f8505d` (peak RSS and a site
  result file) and `e52bbe896` (link each olean beside its source) on
  `teal-sea/rung3-centre-pilot-sep18` fix that. This branch first cherry-picked them; they
  then landed on `main` through PR #323 with a follow-up, `571857a47`, that keeps the
  root-olean link across resumes, and this branch merged `origin/main` (`e08e801da`), so the
  pilot runs `main`'s driver.
- **Not built anywhere.** No script emits the assembly module that combines the 215 site
  theorems with `norm_deriv_DH_le`, `DH_lower_on_hcell`, `DH_lower_on_vcell` and the square
  criterion into `theorem davenport_heilbronn : davenport_heilbronn_statement`.

## Scope

- **In scope.** Lean setup in this worktree's `lean/` by a fresh Lake fetch; building only
  the `ZetaLean` modules the certificate imports; one staged prime tower
  (`scripts/68`); one small big-box site generated by `scripts/60 --arith ball --shard
  --only <site>` and compiled by `scripts/72 --jobs 1 --module-jobs 1` into a directory
  outside the tree; planted faults in copies of the generated files; a cost table and a
  memo asking the owner for a production window.
- **Out of scope.** Any production shard, any site beyond the pilot, the assembly module,
  any edit to `zeta/`, `ontology/`, `harness/` or the certificate's mathematics in
  `lean/ZetaLean/`, and any claim about RH.
- **Compute.** The swarm box (32 vCPU Haswell, 115 GiB, no swap), shared with other
  workflows. Under `CLAUDE.md` compute rule 6 as merged on `main`, Lean and numerics may
  run side by side while `MemAvailable` stays above 20 GiB; this lane waits while it is
  below 30 GiB before any process that may exceed 8 GB, runs pytest with `-n0` or at most
  `-n 4`, caps parallel numeric jobs at 8 processes and waits while the 1-minute load
  average is above 64 before a heavy job. Rule 7: Lake runs only in a `.lake` it fetched
  itself, with every package's git toplevel checked.
