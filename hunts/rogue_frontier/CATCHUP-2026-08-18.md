# Catch-up: what main learned while this campaign ran, and how it reformulates the next wave

Written 2026-08-18 after merging `origin/main` (44 commits) into the
campaign branch. Nothing here is a new result; it is a re-aim.

## 1. A correction main forced on this campaign's own wording

`d799aac` (a referee's catch on Pub 1) records that `F1` is **not** entire:
`F1 x = fKer |x|`, and the `|x|` puts a corner at the origin, so `F1` is
not even differentiable there. What is true is that `fKer`'s series has
infinite radius.

RF-C008 said "every corrected row is entire". That has exactly the same
defect, because `F_kappa(alpha)` is a series in `|alpha|`. Corrected in
`RESULTS_LEDGER.md` and `REPORT.md`: the object with infinite radius is
the coefficient series in the half-line variable, not `F_kappa` itself.
The Gevrey-1/2 tail bound is untouched (it is a statement about
coefficients), and every evaluation this campaign made uses the series on
the half line only. Same class of defect, same week, caught by someone
else's referee: worth recording as calibration, not just as an edit.

## 2. Pub 1 is the quadratic member of this campaign's own open problem

`4710b7e` moved Pub 1 to **unconditional**, kernel-checked, with
`tests/test_pub1_status.py` guarding the grade. Reading
`lean/ZetaLean/Pub1/Setting.lean`, its setting is verbatim the `xi'`
window problem of `hunts/wide_search/RESULTS-xiprime-global-optimality.md`:

    I = [-1/2, 1/2],  F1(x) = |x| - 4x^2 + sum ((k-1)!/(2k)!)(2|x|)^(2k+1),
    (T v)(s) = int_I F1(s-t) v(t) dt,  A = I + T,  w = A^{-1} 1,  c* = <1, w>.

That is a **quadratic** Rayleigh quotient, solved exactly and attained at
`A^{-1}1`.

RF-C003's open question (WIN-GLOBAL section 9) is the **cubic** analogue:
maximise `F(v) = 1 - D2(v) - D3(v)` with `D3` cubic in `v`. Cubicity is
precisely why that arm could only bracket
`0.685287032176998 <= sup F <= 0.892744211644411` instead of solving it.

Measured here (`optimise(kernel='xiprime')` against the RF-C003 window):
the two maximisers are close in shape but genuinely distinct, endpoint
ratios `0.671041` vs `0.705688`, relative L2 distance `0.0443`. Within
the campaign's own family WIN-GLOBAL had already measured the quadratic
member's optimiser as a near neighbour of the cubic optimum (`5.5e-4` in
`D2`, `1.0e-3` in `F`).

**The re-aim.** The next window experiment is not more optimization. It is
whether the Pub 1 theorem *shape* transfers: does the cubic optimum admit
a Pub-1-style characterisation as an explicit operator equation, with the
kernel-checked `A^{-1}1` as its unperturbed term? If it does, the bracket
closes by perturbation around an object that is already kernel-checked,
rather than by numerical search that can only ever report "never
exceeded". That is a different, and much better, question than the one
`REPORT.md` recorded before this merge.

## 3. Two method transfers worth taking

- **Pricing before proving.** Hunt #48 (`r_8c3b94`) priced Erdos-Kac in
  Lean by compiling 43 `#check`s against the pinned toolchain rather than
  grepping, and reported a line-count band plus the blocking lemma. The
  campaign's own "formalize the Bian finite witnesses" idea should be
  priced that way before it is funded.
- **The withdrawal reflex.** `a735965` withdrew NAMED_GAPS G4 because a
  two-sided quantity had been compared against a one-sided one, and says
  plainly that the error was "wrong in our favour". The campaign has one
  structurally similar exposure: the RF-C008 alpha-optimised `0.9578`
  compares an alpha-optimised reading against literature constants whose
  alpha convention was not separately audited. That audit is now a named
  obligation rather than an assumption.
