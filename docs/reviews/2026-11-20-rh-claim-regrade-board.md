**The short version.**

1. *The October headline would be dominated, with one condition.* All five results built on
   the 7/8 half-plane (`docs/39`) have a stronger counterpart in print under RH or GRH (rows D02
   to D06), which overtakes them only if GRH becomes a theorem. For four of the five that is
   clean. For the class-number lists it rests on a fact nobody here has resolved: whether the
   published h <= 2000 list was computed without a further GRH. If it was not, then given the
   claim the lab's h <= 1500 list is the unconditional part of that range, and row D02 changes
   class. One comparison is stale today whatever happens: the range h <= 1500 lies inside a
   published GRH-conditional table to h <= 2000 (correction C1). Until the replay passes, the
   weaker hypothesis is still the lab's.
2. *What the lab would still hold alone.* The kernel-checked Davenport-Heilbronn zero, with a
   finite-height contrast against zeta that is kernel-checked already, and the
   enclosure-carrying Li onset for the same function (R02, R05); the enclosed sign change of
   its Weil form (R12); the kernel-checked simplicity and exact count of the 29 zeros to
   height 100 (U01); the unconditional four-point theorem (D12, dominated in size under RH
   but independent of the claim); the exact class-number sieve that reads no GRH (D02); and
   the replay pipeline (D01).
3. *What the claim would promote.* Two statements of the lab's own carry RH as a written
   hypothesis, and the first build of this board missed both. The distinct-zeros proportion
   0.850828702939872 of `hunts/rogue_frontier/window_opt/` (H08, claim RF-C003) is not dominated
   on the tree's own record: it sits about 2.4e-6 above its source preprint. The xi'' simplicity
   proportion of `hunts/higher_xi/` (H09) keeps its bridge hypotheses. Neither becomes a theorem
   by the claim. H08 rests on a preprint that is not peer reviewed and has had no blind attack;
   H09 has open findings in the lab's own review ledger. For simple zeros of zeta itself the
   lab's constants stay below the RH-conditional 19/27 the tree records (D12 to D15).
4. *What turns over.* The criterion instruments (`criteria`, `li`, `weil`, `zeros` with `rigor`),
   the equivalence catalogue and the Weil Gram hunt stop testing a conjecture and start
   checking a theorem (O01 to O07). In practice less changes than that sentence suggests:
   only three faces of `criteria` can fail in a finite range, and the Robin scan, the Jensen
   table and the zero count were must-pass on their whole reach before the claim. The
   de Bruijn-Newman constant becomes 0 (V01 to V03). Twelve rows are TARGET SETTLED (T01 to
   T12): the mandate, five course pages or groups of pages, five groups of hunts, and the
   scope wording with the tests that pin it.
5. *What the claim leaves open that the lab can reach.* Simplicity, the next family of
   L-functions, the Legendre side, explicit GRH constants and the spacing of the zeros
   (section (b), with a recommended order). Three rows look as if RH fixed a value and do
   not, because the value also needs simple zeros or a second hypothesis (H09, U12, U13).
