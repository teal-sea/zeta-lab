Zero visibility

Each gap between consecutive zeta zeros becomes a node; two gaps are joined when every gap between them is shorter than both (a horizontal visibility graph). For independent data the degree law is a theorem, P(k) = (1/3)(2/3)k−2. The zeros break it, and they break the random-matrix prediction too.
