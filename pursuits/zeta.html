Zeta

A computational and formal investigation around the Riemann hypothesis.

The machinery computes ζ by independent routes and compares them, exposes identities as measured defect functions rather than assuming them, and pins every number claimed in a docstring to a test. Two things here are stronger than “accurate”: interval arithmetic that carries an enclosure through every step, and Lean proofs checked by a kernel.

It claims nothing about whether the hypothesis is true. A computation that appears to settle it is a bug, and the repository says so in its own house rules.
