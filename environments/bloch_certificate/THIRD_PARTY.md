# Third-party material

At runtime this package downloads Frank Wikström's public Zenodo archive,
`bloch-computations-1.0.0.zip`, record `10.5281/zenodo.21975862`, and refuses to
execute it unless its SHA-256 is
`bdaa1ff347043a00733ca40d5db46c5418810d1f4e5c472d0bcb9de48ef408e7`.
The archive is MIT-licensed, copyright 2026 Frank Wikström. Its own `LICENSE`
and `CHECKSUMS.sha256` remain inside the archive. No archive file is copied
into this package or repository.

The wrapper is MIT-licensed, copyright 2026 Thomas Lince. The environment
reports what the archive's programs accept. It does not transfer authorship of
the upstream certificate or claim that the paper's handwritten proof was
audited.
